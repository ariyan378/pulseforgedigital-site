How we work

Smart strategy. Real execution. Measurable impact. Here's the full client journey, step by step.

01

Initial consultation

You message us on WhatsApp with what you're trying to solve. We ask enough questions to understand the shape of the project.

02

Discovery and requirements

We learn about your business, audience, existing data, and what you already have in place.

03

Research and strategy

We work out the approach — which services fit, and how they connect to your goal.

04

Planning

We agree on scope: what's being delivered, in what form, before any work begins.

05

Design or implementation

We build the campaigns, dashboards, automations, or other deliverables agreed on.

06

Review and revisions

We share the work with you and go through any changes needed before it's considered final.

07

Quality assurance

We check the work against what was scoped before it's delivered.

08

Delivery or launch

The finished work is handed over or goes live, depending on the service.

09

Ongoing support

We stay reachable on WhatsApp for questions after delivery.
